StripSnap Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

App: StripSnap (Android), package com.asadgulshair.stripsnap

Developer: Asad Gulshair

Short version

StripSnap does not collect, send, sell, or share any personal data. It has no account, no ads, and no analytics, and it does not use the internet.

What the app uses and where it stays

  • Photos of your test strip. When you take a photo or pick one from your gallery, the app reads the colors on your phone. The photo is not uploaded anywhere. The photo is not saved in your history. Android may keep a temporary copy in the app's private cache on your phone, which only the app can read and which is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.
  • Your settings. Pool or hot tub, water volume, units, strip type, and chlorine strength are saved only on your phone so you don't have to enter them again.
  • Your test history. Past results (date, values, and how sure the reading was) are saved only on your phone so you can see trends. The app keeps up to 60 results.

None of this leaves your device. We (the developer) cannot see it.

Camera and photos

The app opens your phone's own camera app or photo picker only when you tap "Take photo" or "From gallery". It does not access your camera or photos in the background.

Internet, ads, and analytics

The app does not connect to the internet. It contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings > Apps > StripSnap > Storage > Clear storage. This removes all settings and history.

Children

StripSnap is meant for adults who look after pools and hot tubs. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Important note about results

StripSnap gives estimates from a photo. Results can be wrong because of lighting, the camera, or the strip. Always follow the chemical product's label, add chemicals a little at a time, and retest. For important decisions, use a proper test kit or ask a pool professional.

Changes to this policy
